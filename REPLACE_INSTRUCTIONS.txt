2) Open this unzipped folder. Select ALL its files and subfolders, copy them into

   when macOS asks. All scripts, INCLUDING 01_generate_synthetic_data.R, are provided.
   Do NOT delete the existing repo or its hidden .git directory.
3) Open the .Rproj file in the repo, then run once in RStudio:
      source("run_all.R")
